# Smart Notes App

Smart Notes App is an AI-powered productivity tool designed to make note-taking smarter, faster, and more intuitive. With OCR (Optical Character Recognition) powered by Google ML Kit, you can scan handwritten or printed text directly into your digital notebook. Organize, categorize, and manage all your thoughts, ideas, and tasks seamlessly — all within one app.
